01 Who we are

Together, we regenerate.

CYCLE is a youth-led civil society organisation in Guyana. We treat regenerative agriculture, biodiversity, community health, youth and women's enterprise as one system — because a farmer with healthy soil and no market is still poor, and a clinic without nutritious food still treats the same illness twice.

  • Youth-led
  • Rooted in Guyana
  • Caribbean-wide by 2030

02 Our model

The value is in the turn.

Most organisations pick one of these four and call it a strategy. We run all four as a loop — each turn feeding the next.

  1. 1

    Research & Publish

    Field science at Pakuri and Moco Moco, published openly.

  2. 2

    Empower Agripreneurs

    Training and enterprise support for women, Indigenous and youth farmers.

  3. 3

    Showcase & Market

    Their farms and produce taken public, at home and abroad.

  4. 4

    Shape Policy

    Evidence into climate-resilient agriculture policy, Caribbean-wide.

  • Regenerative Agriculture
  • Nutrition & Preventive Health
  • Youth & Environmental Education
  • Women's Enterprise
  • Biodiversity Conservation
